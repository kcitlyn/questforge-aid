# Item 6 — Partner Outreach Email

Tone: **cold but local** — polite first contact, no prior relationship assumed.
Fill the `[BRACKETS]`, delete this header, and you're done.

How to pick a strong partner (so your "why they're a fit" is real):
- **Public libraries** — best default. They already run free youth programs, have
  space + a teen/children's librarian, and *want* low-cost enrichment. Easiest yes.
- **Middle schools / after-school clubs** — great fit for the 8–14 age band and
  the SEL/empathy research in the deck, but slower to approve (district process).
- **Boys & Girls Clubs / community centers** — mission-aligned, flexible, used to
  drop-in facilitators.

Whatever you choose, your "why it's a fit" should name something *specific* about
them (an existing program, their age range, a stated goal) — not generic praise.

### What makes this one land (not a template)
- **Names something only *this* library has** — Teen Central, the music lab, the
  Teen Library Fellowship. That's the line that proves you did real homework.
- **Turns their space into the reason** — "you believe teens learn by making
  things" flatters their philosophy, then ties it to your product. Warm, not
  flattery-for-its-own-sake.
- **Leads with value + a small ask** — free, you run it, 15-minute call. Low risk
  for a cold first contact.
- **Cites the research honestly** — "linked to," never "guarantees."
- **Local touch** — "right by campus" signals you're a real neighbor, not a
  mass-mailer. (All Teen Central details verified from the library site on
  2026-07-23 — re-check before sending.)
