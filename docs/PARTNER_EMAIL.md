# Partner Outreach Email

A draft I'd send to a real local partner. I picked the Austin Central Library
because its Teen Central space (music lab, gaming computers, zine library, a paid
Teen Library Fellowship) shows a team that already believes teens learn by making
things, which is exactly how our games work.

---

To: Kathleen Houlihan, Teen Services (kathleen.houlihan@austintexas.gov)
Subject: A free storytelling program for Teen Central

Hi Kathleen,

I'm Kaitlyn, reaching out from Quest Craft. We build inclusive, educational
role-playing games for young people, and our older adventures are aimed right at
the early-teen range (13-14).

I'm writing to you specifically because of Teen Central. Between the music lab,
the gaming computers, the zine library, and a paid Teen Library Fellowship, it's
clear your team believes teens do their best learning when they get to make
things, not just consume them. That's the same spirit our games run on.

A Quest Craft session is guided, collaborative storytelling. An adult facilitator
leads a small group through an adventure where the players make the choices and
build the story together, and no gaming or prep experience is needed. Programs
like this have been linked to gains in empathy, confidence, and social skills,
which is why more libraries have started folding them into teen programming.

My ask is a small one. I'm developing an AI "co-pilot" that helps a facilitator
stay confident when players go off-script mid-session, and I'd love to playtest it
with a small group of your younger teens over a couple of short sessions. There's
no cost to the library. I'd bring everything and run or co-run the sessions
myself. What I'm really after is your team's read on what actually lands with
Central's teens, since you know that audience far better than I do.

Would a quick 15-minute call make sense to see if this fits your programming? I'm
right by campus and happy to work around your schedule.

Thank you for building a space like Teen Central. It's genuinely the kind of place
this was made for.
